# Lemnaria brand tokens — provisional baseline

These are the previous configurator's fallback brand tokens, retained until a dedicated brand pass
confirms or replaces them. They are not a claim of final brand approval.

## Type, imagery, and voice

Use Inter for UI copy; Fraunces is an optional display companion. Use a 4 px spacing grid,
tabular numerals for price-like data, natural warm photography, real installations where possible,
and visible craft/material texture. The voice is warm, expert, understated, and equally native in
Romanian and Russian—avoid superlatives and translated-sounding copy.
